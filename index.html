Parametrix · Visualization Services

Show the project, not the plan set.

Camera-matched photo simulations, real-time models people can drive through, and a tabletop kit for designing streets with your hands.

Transportation projects rarely stall because the engineering is wrong. They stall because nobody outside the room can picture them. We build the things that let people see it — whether that is one rendering for a grant application, a corridor running live in a game engine at an open house, or a box of snap-together parts on a conference table.

What we make

Photo simulations

Photo-composited renderings

We photograph the site ourselves, model the design from your CAD, and camera-match the two so the proposal sits in the real world — the same light, the same lens, the same afternoon.

  • FAA Part 107 aerial and ground photography
  • Roadway, ramps, striping, signals, lighting, landscaping
  • Draft review on an interactive before/after slider
  • Available as a subscription from four views a year

Hands-on kit

SnapStreets

A snap-together physical kit for building roadway cross-sections on a table. Hand it to a workshop and people stop debating an abstraction and start moving lanes around themselves.

  • Labelled deck modules at true nominal widths
  • Snap-on striping, crosswalks, bike buffers and barrier
  • Lights, signals, PHBs, gantries, soundwall, shelters, trees
  • Ships in Highway and Complete Street configurations
